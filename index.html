Job posting requirements

Companies must provide full shipment details so couriers can quote accurately and safely.

Required job details

  • Load size: length, width, height, and weight.
  • Required vehicle size and load capacity.
  • Collection and delivery addresses with references.
  • Distance between collection and delivery points.
  • Collection and delivery windows with contact names.

Additional requirements

  • Equipment: straps, blankets, sack trucks.
  • Handling notes and fragile or oversized items.
  • Special access or site-specific instructions.
  • Optional internal reference numbers for accounting.
  • Insurance value declarations for higher-risk loads.
